How we measure

Discord members and online counts come from Discord's public invite page for each project's official invite link. Discord itself reports these numbers as approximate.

Telegram counts come from the public t.me page of the project's group or channel. Groups show members and how many are online; channels show subscribers only.

Online numbers are a snapshot at the moment of measurement and change through the day. Discord's approximate counts can also stay unchanged for hours, so we compare several measurements before reading anything into a single unusual value.

We check that each link belongs to the project by the server or channel name it returns. We use no logins, no private data, and no estimates. Twitter/X follower counts and website traffic are not included, because they cannot be measured reliably from public data.
